# KhaleejFlow — The Gulf's Modern News & Media Journal

Inspired by **[Khaleej Weekly](https://khaleejweekly.com/)**, **KhaleejFlow** is a modern, responsive digital news and lifestyle publication focused on the GCC region (**United Arab Emirates 🇦🇪, Saudi Arabia 🇸🇦, Qatar 🇶🇦, Kuwait 🇰🇼, Bahrain 🇧🇭, and Oman 🇴🇲**).

---

## 🌟 New Features & Architectural Highlights

### 1. New Menu Style: Dedicated Category Ribbon
- **Clean Two-Tier Structure**:
  - **Upper Brand Bar**: Logo, E-E-A-T Accreditation pill, compact live search bar, saved reading list button, and theme switcher.
  - **Sticky Category Ribbon**: Elegant, full-width pill navigation that never wraps awkwardly (`Home`, `Featured Stories`, `Plan Your Trip`, `Travel & Aviation`, `Business & Vision 2030`, `Real Estate`, `Lifestyle & Dining`, `Gulf Calculators`).

### 2. Editorial Board & Contributing Experts (E-E-A-T Section)
- Added a dedicated **E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness)** bureau section on the homepage:
  - **Tariq Al-Mansoor** — Senior Real Estate Analyst & DLD Certified Appraiser (Dubai Bureau).
  - **Reem Al-Ghamdi** — Gulf Economic Bureau Chief (Riyadh KAFD Bureau).
  - **Faisal Al-Hashimi** — Cultural Heritage & Travel Lead (Muscat & Doha Bureaus).
  - **Dr. Layla Bint Khalid** — Gastronomy & Contemporary Lifestyle Critic (Doha Msheireb Bureau).
  - **Omar Farooq** — Abu Dhabi Capital Markets Editor (ADGM Bureau).
  - **Zayd Noor** — Aviation & Cross-Border Logistics Specialist (Dubai Aviation City Bureau).
- Displays author avatars, verified contributor badges, bureau locations, bios, published report counts, and direct filter links.

### 3. Dedicated Separate Blog Page (`article.html?id=<id>`)
Every blog post across the entire website now opens on its own dedicated separate page formatted in the exact requested sequence:
1. **First: H1 Title & Header**:
   - Breadcrumbs navigation (`Home / [Category] / [Title]`)
   - Category badge, country flag, publishing date, reading time, and **Verified E-E-A-T Report** seal.
   - Large bold **H1 Headline**.
   - Hero cover image with photo credit caption.
   - Author byline bar with avatar and share actions.
2. **Second: Table of Contents**:
   - Interactive, styled jump-link navigation menu for quick section reading.
3. **Third: Full Content**:
   - In-depth, well-researched reporting with `h2` subheadings, pull quotes, bulleted key takeaways, and market data.
4. **Fourth: Tags & Topics Section**:
   - Pill-shaped tags linking to related topics (`#DubaiRealEstate`, `#Vision2030`, etc.).
5. **Fifth: Author E-E-A-T Section**:
   - Author bio box detailing verified credentials, years of experience, and bureau affiliations.
6. **Sixth: Related Investigations Section**:
   - 3 companion article cards from the same region and category.
7. **Best-in-Class Sidebar**:
   - Author quick card with verification badge
   - Quick WhatsApp & link sharing buttons
   - Trending Now (01–05) ranked articles
   - The Executive Morning daily newsletter signup

### 4. Rich Homepage Sections
- **Magazine Hero Spotlight**: Major spotlight + 2 secondary featured articles.
- **Trending in the Gulf**: 4-card horizontal showcase.
- **Plan Your Trip & Travel Guides**: 4 curated destination guides (AlUla, Musandam, Desert Glamping, Doha).
- **Real Estate & Property Market**: 3-column analysis hub.
- **Editorial Bureau & Experts**: The 6-author E-E-A-T grid.
- **Latest Khaleej Stories Feed & Sidebar**: Grid/List view switcher, interactive UAE Labour Gratuity & RERA Rent Calculators, Trending rankings, and Newsletter.
- **Lifestyle, Culture & Dining**: 4-card curated gallery.
